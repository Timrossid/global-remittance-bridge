# AML/KYC Policy Guide

## Purpose
To prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and other financial crimes.

## Know Your Customer (KYC)
### Identity Verification
- Government-issued photo ID
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement)
- Beneficial ownership information

### Risk Classification
- **Low risk**: Verified individuals, small transaction volumes
- **Medium risk**: Businesses, moderate transaction volumes
- **High risk**: Politically exposed persons (PEP), high transaction volumes

## Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
### Transaction Monitoring
- Real-time monitoring for suspicious patterns
- Large transaction reporting (>€10,000)
- Structuring detection (multiple small transactions)
- Sanctions screening against OFAC, EU, UN lists

### Red Flags
- Unusual transaction patterns
- Reluctance to provide information
- Use of anonymous payment methods
- Transactions with high-risk jurisdictions

## Compliance Measures
- Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk customers
- Ongoing monitoring and review
- Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filing
- Staff training programs
- Independent audit function
