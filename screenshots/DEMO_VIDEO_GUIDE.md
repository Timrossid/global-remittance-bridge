# Demo Video Recording Guide

**Target length:** 2 minutes  
**Resolution:** 1080p minimum  
**Tool:** Loom, OBS Studio, or any screen recorder

---

## Timing Breakdown

| Section | Duration | Action |
|---------|----------|--------|
| 1. Sign-up flow | 0:00–0:30 | Register a new merchant |
| 2. Dashboard overview | 0:30–1:00 | Show stats and recent transactions |
| 3. Payment initiation | 1:00–1:30 | Create a payment and track status |
| 4. Feedback submission | 1:30–2:00 | Submit feedback form |
| Total | 2:00 | — |

---

## Pre-Recording Setup

1. Open https://merchant-dashboard-rosy.vercel.app in a fresh browser window (incognito recommended).
2. Clear any existing session cookies if you want a clean sign-up.
3. Have a test email ready, e.g. `demo+test01@example.com`.
4. Mute notifications and close unrelated tabs.
5. Start your screen recorder and begin narration immediately.

---

## Tips for a Smooth Recording

- Speak clearly and at a moderate pace.
- Keep mouse movements smooth; avoid jerky scrolling.
- If you make a mistake, pause for 2 seconds, then repeat the sentence — you can trim silence later.
- Export as MP4 or WebM, upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Loom, and copy the share link.

---

## After Recording

1. Upload the video to YouTube, Loom, or Google Drive.
2. Copy the shareable link.
3. Add the link to:
   - `README.md` (under **Live Links** or a new **Demo Video** row)
   - `screenshots/DEMO_SUBMISSION_NOTES.md` (at the top, under **Live Demo URLs**)
